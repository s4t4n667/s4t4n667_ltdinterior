## LTD Gas Station Redesign

Redesigned LTD Gas Station interior which changes the interior of all LTD stations across the map. Few quality of life changes, and a modern refresh.

**Changes:**
- changed colour scheme to match logo - orange to red, pale blue to bolder blue
- new flooring, new ceiling, new walls
- removed all dirt off floors
- tucked chair in in the office and removed bin - sometimes gets in the way of robbery scripts
- moved things slightly on the counter for ease of access to register (for RP purposes)
